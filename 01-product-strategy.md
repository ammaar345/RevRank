# 01 — Product Strategy

## The Problem (Honest Assessment)

TripRank has a strong core idea but is currently:
- **A data logger pretending to be a game** — it records trips but gives users no reason to care
- **Paywalling too early** — users hit Pro walls before they understand the product's value
- **Losing identity** — it looks like every other dark-mode GPS app
- **Missing the social hook** — shareable images exist but there's nothing to share *toward*

## Repositioning

**Before:** "Track your trips and see your stats"
**After:** "Your car has a score. What's yours?"

TripRank becomes the **Duolingo for driving** — a skill-building, rank-climbing, socially competitive driving companion. Every drive is a performance. Every performance is ranked. Every rank is shareable.

Target users:
- **Primary:** 18–34 car enthusiasts, commuters, rideshare drivers who want to track their driving quality
- **Secondary:** Parents tracking new drivers, fleet operators (Pro/Enterprise later)
- **Tertiary:** People who just like data about themselves (quantified-self crowd)

---

## Core Value Proposition (Free Tier)

The free tier must feel *complete* for a casual user, or the app dies by review-bombing:

**Free Forever:**
- Unlimited trip tracking (auto-detect)
- Trip score (0–100) per drive
- Rank system (7 tiers)
- Last 10 trips history
- Badges & achievements (all unlockable free)
- Weekly recap card (shareable)
- Basic stats: speed, distance, time, smoothness

**Pro ($3.99/mo or $24.99/yr):**
- Unlimited trip history
- Route replay with color-coded segments
- G-force visualization
- Friends leaderboard
- Custom route naming & tagging
- Advanced analytics (trends over time)
- Export CSV
- Widget (home screen speedometer)
- No ads (when ads are introduced)

**Enterprise (future, $19.99/mo per seat):**
- Fleet dashboard
- Driver scoring for multiple users
- Reports & compliance exports

## Startup Path

### Phase 0 (Now — Week 4): Fix the Foundation
- Fix the username/login recovery bug (Google + Apple Sign-In)
- Fix the paywall hitting on day 2 (enforce free tier properly)
- Fix GPS lag (see performance branch)
- Implement auto-trip detection
- Redesign onboarding (3 screens, set expectations)

### Phase 1 (Week 5–8): Gamification Drop
- Trip scoring system live
- Rank tiers live
- Badges live
- Weekly recap card live
- App Store / Play Store screenshots updated with new branding

### Phase 2 (Week 9–12): Social Layer
- Shareable trip cards (redesigned, matrix aesthetic)
- Friend challenge links
- Global leaderboard (weekly top scores)
- "Beat my score on this route" deep links

### Phase 3 (Month 4–6): Pro Maturity
- Route replay
- Advanced analytics
- Home screen widget
- Referral program ("Give 1 month Pro, get 1 month Pro")

### Phase 4 (Month 7+): Growth & Enterprise
- Fleet/B2B tier
- Driving instructor integration
- Insurance partnership exploration (UBI — usage-based insurance)
- API for third-party integrations

## Competitive Positioning

| App | Weakness | TripRank Advantage |
|-----|---------|-------------------|
| Waze | No driving quality score | TripRank scores every drive |
| Bouncie | Hardware required | Pure software |
| eDriving Mentor | Enterprise only, ugly | Consumer-first, beautiful |
| Automatic | Discontinued | Active, modern |
| Life360 | Surveillance-focused | Performance-focused |

**Moat:** The rank/badge system creates identity. Once someone is "Road Captain Rank 4" they don't want to start over on a competitor.
