# Play Store Screenshots (Phone - Portrait)

## Requirements
- Min 2 screenshots (App Store: max 8), max 8 per device type
- Aspect ratio: 9:16 or 16:9 (portrait preferred for motorcycle apps)
- Minimum size: 320px, Max: 3840px on shortest side
- Supported formats: JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- Must not contain any device frames, operating system logos, or purchase/pricing text (per Play Store policies, pricing is allowed in feature graphics but not in screenshots)

## Recommended captures (8 max)
1. **Trip End Screen (Score Reveal)**
   - Content: Score "87" displayed large, showing 2 green sub-scores and one amber sub-score
   - Shows: Trip map, distance, duration, and share button
   - Why: The "money shot" — immediately communicates the scoring mechanic

2. **Home Dashboard**
   - Content: Greeting, rank progress, weekly stats, last trip summary
   - Shows: The app as a daily-use tool, not just a tracker
   - Why: Gives new users a sense of the full app experience

3. **Live HUD Tracking**
   - Content: Bike icon, current speed, current score, and route line on a dark map
   - Why: Shows the core value proposition — active ride tracking

4. **Ranks Screen**
   - Content: 7-tier rank ladder, "Enforcer" rank mid-screen highlighted
   - Shows: User is ranked "Enforcer" with progress bar to next rank
   - Why: Gamification sells engagement

5. **Trip History**
   - Content: List of past trips with scores, distance, and date
   - Shows: History and progression over time
   - Why: Users want to see data builds up

6. **Shareable Score Card (open share sheet)**
   - Content: Score card preview with score %, breakdown, bike image in circle
   - Shows: Social proof and virality mechanic
   - Why: Users share this, driving organic growth

7. **Analytics Dashboard (Pro)**
   - Content: Scores over time (line chart), distance (bar chart), and radar chart
   - Shows: Pro value, data depth
   - Why: Upsell the Pro tier

8. **Route Replay (Pro)**
   - Content: Map with route overlay, playback controls at bottom
   - Shows: Pro-only route replay feature
   - Why: Visual proof of premium feature

## Safe Areas
- Top 64px: safe (status bar)
- Bottom 32px: safe (navigation bar)
- Left/Right edges: no critical text or UI elements within 24px of edges

## Content Guidelines
- Use realistic demo data (e.g., "87 / 100" for a high-quality and natural looking score rather than unrealistic "0/100" or a rigid/unoptimized "98/100")
- Dates should be current or recent
- Rider names should be generic ("Alex", "Jordan") or app-specific ("Rider_42")
- Scores should be varied (not all 100) to look realistic
