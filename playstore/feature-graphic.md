# Feature Graphic Spec (1024 x 500 px)

## Purpose
Used at the top of the Play Store listing page. Sets the brand tone immediately.

## Design Direction
- Dark background (#000000 primary, or #0A0A0A)
- Centrally placed: RevRank logo (the "R" needle icon) in MatrixGreen (#00FF41), large, with subtle glow blur
- Tagline below: "SCORE EVERY RIDE" in Rajdhani medium, 36px, white
- Subtle HUD elements: thin grid lines at 5% opacity, faint Matrix green radial gradient behind the logo
- Motorbike silhouette or abstract speed lines in very dark grey as texture (optional, keep subtle)

## Composition
- Safe area for text: central 400px wide strip (avoid text in left 200px and right 200px where Play Store overlays)
- Logo centered horizontally, placed in upper-center third
- Tagline below logo, centered, bottom third
- Aspect ratio: 2.05:1

## Image Assets Needed
- RevRank wordmark (use existing Orbitron/Rajdhani logotype)
- Speedometer needle "R" icon (from app icon assets)
- Optional: dark motorbike silhouette or exhaust trail texture

## Notes
- No screenshot content in this image — purely branding/brand promise
- Must not contain any device frames, operating system logos, or purchase/pricing text (per Play Store policies, pricing is allowed in feature graphics but not in screenshots)
- Consider darkening the logos/speedometer icon in the lower corners as a watermark
